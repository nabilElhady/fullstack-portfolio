## 📝 How to Use New Features

### Download Resume:
1. Add your actual PDF resume to `/public/resume.pdf`
2. Button will automatically download it
3. Customize button text in `components/DownloadResume.tsx`

### Update Stats:
1. Edit `components/AnimatedStats.tsx`
2. Change numbers and labels to match your achievements
3. Add/remove stat cards as needed

### Customize Scroll Progress:
1. Color is already brand color (#f7ab0a)
2. Automatically tracks scroll position
3. No configuration needed!
